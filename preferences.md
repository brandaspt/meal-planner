# Household preferences

Source of truth for every plan. Edit freely; routines read it on each run.

## Household

- 2 adults + 1 toddler. Everyone eats the same family meal.
- Portions are counted in adult portions. A family meal ≈ 2.5 portions; a solo lunch = 1.
- Location: Sweden. Metric units (g, dl, tbsp, tsp). Swedish product names in brackets where useful.

## Diet rules

- **Salt:** none added while cooking. Adults season at the table. Prefer unsalted stock, rinsed tinned legumes, low-salt soy; keep bacon, ham, sausage and hard cheese as flavouring, not the main.
- **Sugar:** zero added sugar. Sweetness only from fruit or veg.
- **Fibre:** every meal has vegetables and/or legumes; favour wholegrain (fullkorn) pasta, bread, bulgur, barley, oats, potatoes with skin.
- **Balance, not rules:** rice and wild/Baltic salmon are fine occasionally (rice ≈ once a week max). No toddler warnings or choking notes in recipes.
- **Never use:** garlic (incl. garlic powder), tofu, coriander (leaf and seed), aubergine, halloumi. Avoid spice blends that usually contain garlic or coriander (curry powder, garam masala, taco mix); build flavour from single spices instead. Asafoetida (hing) + ginger replace garlic in Indian dishes.

## Protein mix per week

| Protein | Cooks per week |
|---|---|
| Fish / seafood | 2 |
| Poultry | 2–3 |
| Red meat | 1–2 |
| Vegetarian (legumes, eggs) | 1, max 2 |
| Processed meat | rarely, as flavouring only |

## Cuisines

Favourites: Mediterranean and Asian. Indian (quick, garlic-free) roughly every 1–2 weeks. Swedish husmanskost and Mexican welcome.

## Cooking

- Time: 30–45 min per cook. Saturday lunch ≤ 30 min. Friday "assisted" ≤ 20 min.
- Appliances: two air fryers (preferred — quick, little mess), stove, oven.
- Recipes: concise. Ingredients + 3–6 short steps. No preamble.

## Week pattern (Sat → Fri)

| Day | Lunch | Dinner |
|---|---|---|
| Sat | Cook, quick (≤ 30 min) | **Big cook** (makes extra) |
| Sun | Leftovers of Sat dinner | **Big cook** (makes extra) |
| Mon | Solo leftovers | Leftovers / freezer night |
| Tue | Solo leftovers | Cook |
| Wed | Solo leftovers | Cook |
| Thu | Solo leftovers | Cook |
| Fri | Solo leftovers | Assisted-quick (e.g. fiskpinnar in air fryer + fresh side) |

- Weekday solo lunch = previous day's dinner, so weekday dinners make one extra portion.
- Avoid the same dish in 3+ consecutive meals for the adult eating solo lunches; alternate Sat/Sun leftovers if needed.
- Aim to put ~1 portion per week into the freezer as a buffer for Monday.
- Assisted-quick meals: pick lower-salt products (< 1 g salt / 100 g where possible).

## Variety

- ~70% from `favourites.md` (once it has entries), ~30% new dishes.
- No dish repeated within 3 weeks. Never plan anything listed under "Skip".
- Prefer seasonal Swedish produce (e.g. autumn/winter: root veg, cabbage, kale, leeks, apples).

## Shopping

- **Coop (online):** every 2nd week, delivered Tuesday. Pantry, frozen and long-life items for two weeks.
- **ICA (in store):** weekly, Fri–Sun. Fresh items for the coming week, plus anything the Coop order didn't cover.
- Group lists by store section. Quantities rounded to what the shop sells.
